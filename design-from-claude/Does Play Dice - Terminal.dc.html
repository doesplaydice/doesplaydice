# the one page

## roll
Say what you do. Roll a d20. Meet or beat the difficulty and it happens.
## three pillars
Body, Mind, Spirit. Each starts at 10. Spend them; rest to recover.
## help
A friend at the table can add their die once per scene.
## harm
Injuries subtract from a pillar until healed. At zero, you are out.
## grow
Each session, raise one pillar by one. Nothing you learned changes.
## run it
The table decides what is true. The referee decides what is hard.
