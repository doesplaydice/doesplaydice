Does Play DiceTHE ONE PAGE · v1.0
Roll. Say what you do. Roll a d20. Meet or beat the difficulty and it happens.
Three pillars. Body, Mind, Spirit. Each starts at 10. Spend them; rest to recover.
Help. A friend at the table can add their die once per scene.
Harm. Injuries subtract from a pillar until healed. At zero, you are out.
Grow. Each session, raise one pillar by one. Nothing you learned changes.
Run it. The table decides what is true. The referee decides what is hard.
