Player feedback slips. Eight per sheet. Cut on the dashed lines and drop one in front of each player at the end of a session — three questions, half a minute, no phone required.

Paper on purpose: it works at a library table tonight with zero setup, and nobody has to be talked into scanning anything. Photograph the filled slips before you leave.
