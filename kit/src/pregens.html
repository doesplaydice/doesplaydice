Pre-generated characters. Print, cut on the dashed lines, hand one to each player. Ready to play with no character creation — useful when someone sits down mid-convention and you have forty minutes.

Why these six: there are exactly six ways to deal three different dice across three skills, and these are all six. Nobody at the table duplicates anybody, and every approach to a problem has someone at the table who is good at it. Swap the trio (here d4 / d6 / d12) and the same six shapes still hold.

The names and backgrounds are samples. They are deliberately setting-neutral, since the setting question is still open. Replace them with your own — the structure is the part that matters.
