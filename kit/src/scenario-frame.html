One-shot frame. A single page for planning a convention session. This is structure, not content — the scenario itself is the author's half, and it is deliberately left blank. The prompts come from the published rules: three ways into every problem, a difficulty die for each, and a tie that gives both sides something.

Print a stack. Fill one in per table.

After the session — what actually happened
