Table tent. Print single-sided on card stock, cut nothing, fold once along the dashed centre line and stand it up. Both faces read upright, so players on either side of the table can see the core mechanic without asking.

Does Play Dice

doesplaydice.com

Describe what you want to do. If it's hard, roll for it. Highest roll wins.

Your three skills
PhysicalStrength, senses, fine motor skills, stamina
MentalKnowledge, memory, reasoning, aptitude
SocialIntuition, influence, charisma, empathy

You have one die for each. Pick the skill that fits how you want to solve it — there's usually more than one way in.

What the Narrator rolls
d4Easy
d6Moderate
d8Challenging
d12High difficulty
d20"Impossible"

Tie? Both sides get something. You shoulder the door open — and hurt your shoulder doing it.
