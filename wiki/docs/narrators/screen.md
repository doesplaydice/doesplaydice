<section class="dpd-screen-block" markdown>
<span class="dpd-screen-label">How hard is it?</span>

| Die | Difficulty |
| --- | --- |
| **d4** | Easy |
| **d6** | Moderate |
| **d8** | Challenging |
| **d12** | High difficulty |
| **d20** | "Impossible" |

Both sides roll. Highest wins. **A tie means both sides get something** &mdash; and that
happens 1 &divide; the larger die of the time, so it is common at the low end and rare at the
high end. [The full odds &rarr;](../rules/odds.md)
</section>

<section class="dpd-screen-block" markdown>
<span class="dpd-screen-label">The four Canons</span>

When a call is genuinely unclear, these decide it. They are Jeff's, verbatim.

1. **Finality** &mdash; the GM is the final arbiter.
2. **Fun** &mdash; seek collaborative and fun solutions.
3. **Consistency** &mdash; outcomes should be predictable. Even if they aren't predicted.
4. **Universality** &mdash; whatever a player can do, all players can do. Corollary: whatever
   the players can do, enemies can do.
</section>

<section class="dpd-screen-block" markdown>
<span class="dpd-screen-label">The three Skills</span>

| Skill | Reach for it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical** | Strength, senses, fine motor skills, stamina |
| **Mental** | Knowledge, memory, reasoning, aptitude |
| **Social** | Intuition, influence, charisma, empathy |

If a player argues their approach fits a different Skill and the argument is good, it fits.
That is the Canon of fun doing its job.
</section>

<section class="dpd-screen-block" markdown>
<span class="dpd-screen-label">When you are stuck</span>

- **Say what they see, not what it means.** Description before interpretation.
- **Ask a player.** "What does your character notice first?" costs nothing and buys a minute.
- **Let the dice decide, then narrate backwards.** Roll first, explain after.
- **A tie is a gift.** It is the one outcome where you get to say yes *and*.
- **When in doubt, escalate the situation, not the difficulty.**
</section>
