Running a game is the harder half, and it's the half that decides whether someone plays once or
plays for years. This section would cover setting scenes, calling for checks, pacing a night,
and prepping little so you can improvise a lot.

## What exists in Jeff's notes

There's more finished thinking here than anywhere else in the rules material:

- **`DM advice.md`** — 83,000 characters of collected guidance
- **`Monster generator.md`** — 80,000 characters
- **Adventure hooks, clues, encounters, creating interesting NPCs** — drafted
- **`100 Warmup Questions.md`**, **`New Player Introduction.md`** — table-ready
- **The Canons of Construction** — Jeff's four interpretation principles, and the most
  finished piece of design philosophy in the folder:

!!! quote "From `Playing the Game.md`"

    1. **Canon of finality** — the GM is the final arbiter.
    2. **Canon of fun** — seek collaborative and fun solutions.
    3. **Canon of consistency** — outcomes should be predictable. Even if they aren't predicted.
    4. **Canon of universality** — whatever a player can do, all players can do. Corollary: whatever the players can do, enemies can do.
