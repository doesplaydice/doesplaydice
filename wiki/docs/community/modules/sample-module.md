**For:** 3&ndash;5 players, one session, no experience needed.
**Dice:** the Narrator hands out d4, d6, d12.

## The premise

The party is escorting a merchant's cart through country that would rather they didn't. A tree
has come down across the road. That's it. That's the adventure &mdash; everything after this is
what the players decide to do about it.

## Why it works as a first session

There is no correct answer, and every Skill has a way in:

| Approach | Skill | The Narrator's difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift the tree by main force | Physical | **d12** &mdash; high difficulty, alone |
| Field-modify the cart for the sand alongside the road | Mental | **d8** &mdash; challenging |
| Talk the whole party into hauling together | Social, then Physical | **d6** once six people pull |

That last row is the lesson, and it teaches itself: the hardest roll became the easiest one
because someone thought to ask for help. New players work this out in about four minutes and
never forget it.

## Running it

1. Describe the road, the cart, the merchant's nerves. Ask what they do.
2. Say yes to anything reasonable. Call for a roll only when failure would be interesting.
3. On a tie, give both sides something &mdash; the tree shifts *and* the axle cracks.
4. When they're through, ask what the merchant was really carrying.
