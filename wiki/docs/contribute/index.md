# How to contribute

The point of this project is that the game belongs to the people who play it. Write rules,
settings, adversaries, adventures, art — and keep what you make. No half measures: apart from the
name and logo, [the whole game is open](../licence.md#no-half-measures), and nothing is held back as closed content.

## Why it's built this way {.dpd-display}

Three ideas sit behind the game: anyone can take it, a first-timer and a veteran can share a
table, and five dice are the whole universe. One page, with further reading for each.

## How editing actually works {.dpd-display}

There are two ways in, depending on how comfortable you are with the tools.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-pencil: **The easy door**

    ---

    Every page has an edit pencil in the top right. Click it, make your change, and it's sent
    to us as a pull request. No install, no command line.

-   :material-source-fork: **The savvy door**

    ---

    The whole site is plain markdown files in a git repository. Clone it, fork it, mirror it,
    edit it in Obsidian, send a pull request. Nothing here is locked up.

</div>

## What happens to your change {.dpd-display}

Every change, through either door, arrives as a pull request. We read each one and decide
whether it fits the game. If it doesn't, nothing stops you: the licence lets you publish your
own version, changed however you like.

Whatever you send is shared under the same terms as the rest of the game,
[CC BY 4.0](../licence.md). You keep the credit for what you wrote.
