## What "launch" has to mean

The one-page rules are already finished. So the launch does **not** depend on the campaign rules
being done. That's deliberate &mdash; it's what keeps the date safe.

=== "Must ship"

    Not at risk. All of it either exists or is nearly done.

    - A real website at an address you can say out loud
    - The one-page rules, properly typeset
    - A downloadable PDF of the rules
    - The printed kit, in hand
    - The licence and trademark notice
    - A way for players to give feedback

=== "Should ship"

    Depends on Jeff's writing between now and October.

    - Campaign rules, clearly marked **playtest draft**
    - Character templates for a few levels
    - A one-shot scenario written by Jeff

=== "Not shipping"

    Explicitly out of scope for this launch. Not cancelled &mdash; just later.

    - Timaeus and the wider world
    - A character vault or app
    - The Foundry virtual-tabletop system
    - Full level 1&ndash;20 character templates

!!! tip "Why the campaign rules can ship as a draft"

    At a convention, "here's the finished game, and here's the part I'm still testing &mdash;
    want to help?" is an *invitation*, not an apology. It gives strangers a reason to talk to
    you, and it means the writing schedule can't sink the launch.
