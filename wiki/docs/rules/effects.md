## Jeff already decided this

The whole file lists what "effects" would have to cover &mdash; *"food, alcohol, drugs,
enhancements, implants, tattoos, curses, blessings, poisons, magical effects, disability"*
&mdash; and then answers it in one sentence:

!!! quote "From `Effects.md`"

    *"These could all be flavor on top of a simple advantage/disadvantage system."*

**That is the chapter.** Everything else here is working out what it implies.

## The whole system

Anything that helps you, anywhere, does one of two things:

- **Roll twice, keep the better one.** Blessed, prepared, well-fed, well-armed, well-advised.
- **Roll twice, keep the worse one.** Poisoned, cursed, exhausted, terrified, in the dark.

Nothing modifies a number. Nothing stacks into a calculation. If two things help and one
hinders, you are helped &mdash; **count the directions, not the sources.**

This is already how the one page works, and how [Taking actions](taking-actions.md) spends
extra effort. There was never a second system to build.

## The one place it is more than flavour

A **step up or down the die ladder** is the heavier version, and Jeff proposes it himself:

!!! quote "From `Pillars - Tactical.md`"

    *"Rolling high means rolling a die level higher than your ability would normally allow. Ex.
    If you roll high on STR checks and your physical ability uses a d6, you can roll a d8
    instead. Rolling low is the opposite. If your ability is a d20, rolling high means rolling
    with advantage. If your ability is a d4, rolling low means rolling with disadvantage."*

Reserve that for things that genuinely change what you are capable of &mdash; a masterwork
tool, a god's attention, a limb you no longer have &mdash; rather than for a good meal.

## Disability is not an effect

The largest part of that 221-word file is not a mechanic. It is a list: blindness, deafness,
being mute, OCD, ADD, DID, PTSD, bipolar, sleep disorders, eating disorders, body dysmorphia,
misophonia, autism. And immediately after it, a second list &mdash; sign languages, a combat
wheelchair, ramps, prosthetics, braille, hearing aids.

!!! quote "From `Effects.md`"

    *"The goal is to make an inclusive game, which means that disability is considered and
    integrated into the game in a respectful and meaningful way."*

**So disability does not belong in this chapter, and this draft proposes taking it out.** A
disability is not a curse or a poison, and putting it in a list with them &mdash; each granting
disadvantage &mdash; would say something Jeff plainly does not mean.

Where it belongs instead is character creation: part of who someone is, alongside background
and connections. If it touches the dice at all, it does so the way armour does &mdash; already
inside the Skill die, explained by the player. A character in a combat wheelchair who has
trained for it has the Physical die their training earned.

**Question for Jeff:** that is a reading of one sentence of intent, and it is his call. Did he
want these in the rules, or in the world?
