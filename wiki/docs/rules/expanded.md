## What would live here

Jeff's notes contain substantial work toward each of these. None of it is settled yet, and
several sections have **two or more competing versions** in the source material.

| Section | State in Jeff's notes |
| --- | --- |
| Taking actions / action economy | 113,000 characters of research. Multiple competing models |
| Combat and injury | At least three versions. The April 2024 draft has unfinished steps |

!!! decision "OPEN DECISION — this is the expensive one"

    There are roughly **295,000 words** of design notes across Jeff's folder, and they
    disagree with each other. `RPG dev notes.md` alone contains around **fifteen different
    attribute schemes** — Body/Mind/Soul, Health/Fitness/Left-brain/Right-brain,
    STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, Heart/Soul/Brains/Guts, and more.

    Choosing between them is **authorship, not engineering**, and it's the single largest
    unbounded cost in the project.

    **The proposal:** we build the machine that publishes chapters. Jeff decides which version
    of each rule is canon, and writes it. Chapters ship as they're finished, not all at once —
    so launch doesn't wait on the whole book.

    **Question for Jeff:** does that split work for you? And which one or two expansions
    matter most for the first six months?
